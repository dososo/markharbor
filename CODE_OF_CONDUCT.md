# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want MarkHarbor to be a practical, respectful open source project. Participants are expected to communicate clearly, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Share reproducible details when reporting bugs.
- Protect private bookmark content, screenshots, exports, and account information.
- Accept maintainers' scope decisions when a request is outside the project boundary.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Posting another person's private information.
- Uploading private X bookmark exports without consent.
- Repeatedly demanding unsupported features after a maintainer has closed the scope discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from the project.
